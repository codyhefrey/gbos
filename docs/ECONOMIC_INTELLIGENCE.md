# GBOS Economic Intelligence v0.1

Status: research and product requirements; not a functioning forecasting or pricing system.

## Capabilities
- Unit economics: contribution margin, CAC, LTV, break-even and working-capital cycles.
- Price elasticity estimation with confidence intervals and experiment tracking.
- Demand forecasts with seasonality, uncertainty and calibration reports.
- Capital allocation scenarios using risk-adjusted cash flows and explicit assumptions.
- Supply-demand, currency and inflation stress tests with source timestamps.
- Market entry comparison and supply-chain disruption simulations.

## Guardrails
- Recommendations are not guaranteed profits or financial advice.
- Pricing controls require review for competition, consumer and sector-specific law.
- Distinguish observed data, assumptions, predictions and actual outcomes.
- Financial actions require explicit permission and human approval.
- Measure model drift, bias, error and forecast calibration.
